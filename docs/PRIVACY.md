# Setflow privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Setflow does not have accounts and does not collect, store on a server, sell or share personal data.

**What stays on your phone:** your settings, library list, ratings, playlists and the music files you add. They are not uploaded anywhere.

**What Setflow asks the internet for:** to find free music and radio it sends search terms (genre, tempo range, track names) to Jamendo, the Internet Archive, Radio Browser, and to The Metropolitan Museum of Art for pictures. These services receive your device's IP address as any website does and have their own privacy policies. If you connect your own Mac, the connection stays on your home network.

**Files and storage:** Setflow reads music files you choose, including from Google Drive, Dropbox or other cloud apps through the phone's file picker. It saves free tracks it fetches to its own folder, and listen-only tracks are removed after they are played.

**Permissions:** storage or media access to read your music; internet access to fetch free music, radio and pictures.

**Children:** Setflow is not directed at children under 13.
