# Setflow for labels

Setflow is an Android DJ app. It builds a set from music and blends tracks in time, matching tempo and beat.

**What a label gets**
- Label, artist and track credit shown each time a track plays
- A Buy link on every track, going to your Bandcamp or shop
- A featured label page in the app

**What a label controls**
- Which tracks are included, and whether they are full play or listen-only
- Removal of any or all tracks at any time
- No free downloads unless the label chooses

**Cost to the label:** nothing.

**How it works for listeners:** DJs build a set from their own music, the free catalogues and any partner labels. Partner tracks are credited and linked back to you.
